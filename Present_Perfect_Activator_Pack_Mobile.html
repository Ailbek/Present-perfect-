Present Perfect: Experience, Results and Time Up to Now

Model text · 18 key forms · 50 exercises (Uzbek instructions)
Lesson focus

Use the Present Perfect to connect the past with now: life experience, recent results and time that is not finished yet.

Form & use

Form = have / has + past participle
Experience – ever, never · Result or news – just, already, yet · Time up to now – for, since, so far, recently

Model text Key forms Exercises Write

Dilnoza has studied English for eight months, and she has already made a lot of progress. She has never been abroad, but she has met many foreigners online. Since January, she has watched two English series, and she has read five short books.

Her teacher has just given her a new challenge: a speaking test next week. She hasn't prepared for it yet, but she has written a list of useful phrases. Dilnoza has lost her notebook, so she has bought a new one.

So far, she has learned more than 300 new words, and she has improved her pronunciation, too. Her friends have told her that she has changed a lot. She is very proud of what she has achieved.

Present Perfect or Past Simple?

Finished time (yesterday, in 2019, last summer) = Past Simple. Result or time up to now = Present Perfect.

  3. We ______ to Samarkand last summer.
  4. Ali ______ his homework, so he can play now.
  5. I ______ that film in 2019.
